# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in crproxy to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in technical disagreement.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information, credentials, customer data, or security details without authorization.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, chat spaces, events, and any other forum where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the private contact method listed on their repository profile. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented internally, and avoid conflicts of interest; a maintainer named in a report must not participate in deciding that report.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
